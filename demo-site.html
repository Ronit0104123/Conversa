FAQ

Common questions

Still have questions? Chat with us — we usually reply in under 3 minutes.

What does Northwind cost?

Northwind offers three tiers. Starter is $299/month and covers up to 500 shipments per month with 5 carrier integrations and 3 user seats. Growth is $899/month for up to 5,000 shipments, unlimited carrier integrations, 15 seats, and access to route optimization. Enterprise is custom-priced and includes dedicated infrastructure, SLA guarantees, custom integrations, and a named customer success manager. All plans include a 14-day free trial — no credit card required.

Which ERP and WMS systems do you integrate with?

Northwind ships native connectors for SAP S/4HANA, SAP ECC, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Infor CloudSuite, and Epicor. On the WMS side, we support Manhattan Associates, Blue Yonder, HighJump, and 80+ others. For systems not on this list, our open REST API and webhook infrastructure let your engineering team build a custom integration in 2–4 hours using our documented SDK.

How long does onboarding take?

Most teams complete the initial setup — connecting carriers, configuring their primary shipping lanes, and inviting users — in under 30 minutes using our guided wizard. Historical shipment data backfill (up to 24 months) runs automatically in the background and typically completes within 2 hours. Our onboarding team offers a complimentary live session for Growth and Enterprise customers to walk through carrier authentication, exception rule configuration, and dashboard customization.

What kind of customer support do you provide?

Starter customers get email support with a guaranteed 24-hour response time and access to our self-serve documentation and video library. Growth customers receive priority email and in-app chat support with a 4-hour response SLA during business hours (9am–6pm ET). Enterprise customers get 24/7 phone and chat support, a dedicated Slack channel with their Northwind CSM, and a 1-hour critical-issue response SLA. All customers have access to our public status page and incident postmortems.
