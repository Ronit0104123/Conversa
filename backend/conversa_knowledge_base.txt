## What is Conversa?

Conversa is an AI chatbot platform that helps businesses capture leads and answer customer questions automatically — 24/7, without any human effort.

You give Conversa your website URL. Our AI scrapes and indexes your content, then creates a custom chatbot that knows everything on your site. Visitors can ask it anything, and it responds using your actual website content. When a visitor shows interest, Conversa naturally captures their name, email, and phone number for your team to follow up.

---

## How It Works — 3 Simple Steps

**Step 1: Paste your URL**
Enter your website address (or upload a document/PDF). Conversa will read and index all the content.

**Step 2: AI trains your chatbot**
Our engine processes your content and builds a custom chatbot in seconds. No manual configuration needed.

**Step 3: Embed the script**
Copy and paste one line of code onto your website. Your chatbot is live instantly.

Total setup time: under 2 minutes.

## Features

### AI Responses
Conversa uses advanced natural language understanding to answer visitor questions accurately — in a natural, conversational tone. The responses are based on your actual website content, so nothing is made up.

### Automatic Lead Capture
When a visitor shows buying intent (asks about pricing, wants a demo, asks how to get started), Conversa naturally asks for their contact information. It collects names, emails, and phone numbers in the flow of conversation — no popups, no forms.

### Analytics Dashboard
See exactly what your visitors are asking most. Track conversation volume, common topics, lead conversion rates, and more. Understand your customers better than ever.

### Fast Setup
From entering your URL to having a live chatbot on your site takes under 2 minutes. No developers needed.

### Customization
Match Conversa to your brand. Customize colors, the chatbot's name, its tone and personality, and its position on your site.

### Secure
All data is encrypted with enterprise-grade security. Visitor conversations are private and protected.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long does setup take?**
Under 2 minutes from signing up to having a live chatbot on your website.

**Do I need a developer to install it?**
No. You paste one line of code into your website's HTML. If you use WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, or similar platforms, there are simple ways to add the script without touching code.

**What if my website content changes?**
You can re-train your chatbot anytime from the dashboard. It will scrape your latest content and update its knowledge.

**What kind of leads does it capture?**
Conversa captures name, email address, and phone number. All leads appear in your dashboard and can be exported.

**Does it work in multiple languages?**
Yes. Conversa understands and responds in the language the visitor uses.

**Is my data secure?**
Yes. All conversations and lead data are encrypted and stored securely. We never share your data with third parties.

**Can I customize what the chatbot says?**
Yes. You can edit the chatbot's tone, personality, and even write a custom system prompt from the dashboard.

**What if a visitor asks something the chatbot doesn't know?**
Conversa will politely say it doesn't have that information and offer to have someone from the team reach out — turning uncertainty into a lead opportunity.

**Is there a free plan?**
Conversa is completely free to use right now. No credit card required. No hidden fees.

**How do I get support?**
